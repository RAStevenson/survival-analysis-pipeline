**Endings near the cutoff are provisional.** Where no cancellation exists, a
license's end can only be its latest recorded expiry. Inevitably, a license whose
term ran out shortly before the cutoff but renewed late cannot be told apart
from a real closure. As such, it is recorded as an observed closure. Deaths dated close to the cutoff
therefore include some licenses that will turn out to have survived. The only way
to know is to continually repull and update the dataset as time passes.
