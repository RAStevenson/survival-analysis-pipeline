Each row is one subject in the Mayo Clinic serum free light chain study,
observed from their blood sample until death or the end of follow-up. The
features are the intake measurements: age at sampling, sex, the kappa and
lambda free light chain concentrations, serum creatinine, and the assay's
group decile. Only @val{dataset.event_rate:.0%} of subjects died during
follow-up. For everyone else, the data records how long they had lived so
far, and nothing more.

The recorded start date is a year, with no month or day. Two subjects
sampled in the same year therefore cannot be put in time order, and the
limitations section says what that does to the folds.
